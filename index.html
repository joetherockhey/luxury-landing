Luxury Escapes landing page viewed on different screens

The landing page looks great on a regular laptop screen, where the left-hand margin takes up 5% of the screen’s width. On a larger monitor, though, the left-hand margin grows to 20% of the screen, and the page starts to look a little off. Scroll and tap inside each screen to see the page on a laptop, on a large monitor today, and how it could look if adjusted.

What I think is going on

The content and the offer carousels stay the same width no matter how big the screen is. The page centres a fixed-width content column, and the carousels still run off the right-hand edge as designed. So on a large monitor the right side looks full, but you're left with a big empty margin on the left: 20% of the screen's width, compared with 5% on a laptop.

The fix: the site already knows how wide the browser window is, so the layout can respond to it. Setting the side margins as a percentage of the window width (say 5%), or adding a breakpoint for wide screens, would let the content and carousels grow with the screen. A large monitor would then show about 3.5 offers per row instead of 2.7, and the content would line up with the logo and menu bar.
