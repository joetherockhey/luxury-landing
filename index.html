Luxury Escapes landing page on different screens

Same page, same content width. On a 27″ monitor the empty side margin grows from 5% to 20% of the screen. Scroll and tap inside each screen.

What I think is going on

The content sits in a fixed column about 1140px wide, centred. Every extra pixel of screen goes into the side margins: about 70px each side on the laptop, about 390px on the 27″. The header still runs edge to edge, so the logo and the content no longer line up, and the offer rows show the same 2.7 cards as on a laptop.

One fix: size the margins as a share of the screen (5%) instead of fixing the column. Big screens then show about 3.5 offers per row and the page lines up with the header.
